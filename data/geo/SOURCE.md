# Geography

- `india_outline.geojson`: the India composite boundary, as officially claimed, simplified at 0.01°.
- `coal_states.geojson`: the ten coal-bearing states (property `ST_NM`), simplified at 0.01°.

Source: DataMeet community maps (https://github.com/datameet/maps), based on Survey of India boundaries. Credit
"DataMeet" on the Sources page and check the licence in that repository before publishing.
