                               UNSTARRED QUESTION NO. 4259
                                TO BE ANSWERED ON 26.03.2025

                                 OPERATIONAL COAL MINES

4259. Shri Asaduddin Owaisi:

Will the Minister of Coal be pleased to state:

a) the list of currently operational coal mines in the country along with the estimated reserves for
   each mine;
b) the list of defunct coal mines in the country along with the year in which each mine became
   defunct;
c) whether any measures are being undertaken for the redevelopment of these defunct mines or for
   sections within operational mines that are currently not in use; and
d) if so, the details thereof and if not, the reasons therefor?


                                                 ANSWER

                               MINISTER OF COAL AND MINES
                                  (SHRI G. KISHAN REDDY)

(a): The list of operational coal mines in the country along with their extractable reserves as on
31.03.2024 is attached at Annexure-I

(b): As on 31.3.2024, there are 341 discontinued/abandoned coal mines in the Country, list attached at
Annexure-II.

(c) & (d): Coal Companies have undertaken several measures to make more efficient use of abandoned
& closed coal mines like re-opening of mines, Plantation, Solar Power Plants on OB dumps, Eco parks,
Eco tourism, Ground water recharging & Pump Storage Plants, etc.
